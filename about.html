About SehatPharm

A neighbourhood pharmacy built on one simple promise: genuine medicines, honest prices, no shortcuts.

Our promise

SehatPharm opened in Gulberg, Lahore, to solve a frustration every family knows: wondering whether the medicine you bought is genuine, and what it should really cost. We stock only from licensed distributors, print the price before you pay, and have a qualified pharmacist on duty to answer questions and check every prescription order.

  • Genuine stock from licensed distributors only
  • Clear PKR prices — no hidden charges
  • Pharmacist-checked prescription orders
  • Careful packing: heat-sensitive items in insulated bags
